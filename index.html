Privacy Policy — Verso

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Verso (“the app”) is a comic and book reader for Android, built to work offline. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Verso does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no advertising, and no tracking of you or your reading. What you add to the app stays on your device unless you export it yourself or use one of the optional features described below.

There is one such feature: text recognition (OCR), which is off unless you turn it on. It uses Google’s ML Kit, which sends Google anonymous statistics about how the feature performs. Your pages and the text found in them are not part of that. The section below sets out exactly what is sent.

What the app stores, and where

Verso keeps the following on your device, in its private app storage:

  • A reference to each comic or book you import — its location on your device or in your storage provider — together with a cover image extracted from it. The file itself stays where you put it. Verso does not copy it into the app, and does not move or modify it.
  • Metadata you add, or that is read from the file: titles, authors, tags, collections, notes, ratings.
  • Your reading progress, bookmarks, and reading-session history, which power the statistics and reading-goal features.
  • Your preferences: theme, reading filters, default reading direction, goals.

None of this is transmitted by the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not collect

  • No personal identifiers, accounts, or sign-in.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash reporting of our own. Nothing about you or your reading reaches us — including the ML Kit statistics described below, which go to Google rather than to us.
  • No access to contacts, or to your photo library beyond the files you pick.

On-device text recognition (OCR)

Verso can recognise the text on a page and re-typeset it for readability. This feature is off by default and runs only if you enable it.

It uses Google’s ML Kit, which performs the recognition on your device. Your page images and the recognised text are processed locally and are not uploaded.

ML Kit does send Google metrics about the performance and utilisation of the recognition API — how often it runs, how long it takes. Google states that the data you pass to ML Kit, here your page images, is processed on-device and is not sent to Google. We cannot switch these metrics off and we have no access to them. Google’s handling of them is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and the ML Kit Terms.

If you would rather none of this happens, leave text recognition off. Everything else in the app works without it.

Permissions

These are the permissions the installed app declares. Several come from the libraries Verso is built on rather than from the app itself, and are listed for completeness.

  • Internet access (INTERNET) — not used to transmit your library, reading data, or any personal information. The app works fully offline. It is present because the app framework requires it, and because ML Kit uses it to send the statistics above when OCR is enabled.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — declared by Google’s ML Kit libraries to check connectivity. Verso does not read it.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — declared by the file-handling library the app uses. These are legacy permissions with no effect beyond Android 12. Verso reads the files you choose through Android’s document picker and writes only to its own private storage; it does not browse your device.

Verso requests no runtime permissions, so you should never see a permission prompt from it. There is no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo-library access.

Backups you make

The app offers a manual “Backup library” feature. When you use it, Verso writes a backup file to a location you choose through the Android file picker. You control where it goes and who can reach it. The app does not upload backups anywhere, and restoring one reads only the file you select.

Android’s own automatic backup is switched off for this app, so your library is not copied to your Google account.

Children’s privacy

Verso does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

There is nothing held by us to delete. To remove everything from your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

Deleting a book inside the app removes Verso’s record of it — the metadata, cover, progress and bookmarks. The original file is left untouched, because Verso only ever held a reference to it.
